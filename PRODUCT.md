# Aceone — Product Context

## Register
brand

## Product
Aceone is a financial intelligence blog and newsletter platform for young Indians (22–35). Clean, opinionated, no-jargon financial writing. Paired with a weekly newsletter "The Aceone Brief". Built on Next.js + Payload CMS.

## Target Users
Young Indian professionals (22–35) — first jobs, first investments, first loans. Financially curious but not expert. Mobile-first browsing habits.

## Primary Surface
Blog home (`/`), Article pages (`/posts/[slug]`), Brief archive (`/brief`)

## Brand Personality
Terminal · Stark · Legible

- Space Grotesk (sans) + Space Mono (mono)
- Zero border-radius everywhere
- Grid/border-separator based layout
- Accent: `#3B3FD8` (light) / `#6b6ff0` (dark)
- Design token prefix: `--ao-*`

## Anti-References
- No rounded "fintech friendly" cards
- No gradients
- No warm-neutral cream backgrounds (our bg is #f8f7f4 — barely tinted)
- No stock photo hero imagery
- No SaaS-blue corporate look

## Accessibility
WCAG AA minimum. Mobile-first responsive required.

## Strategic Design Principles
1. Border-first layout — borders ARE the structure, not decoration
2. Mono for metadata, sans for content — never swap
3. Dark footer always, page background toggles
4. 32px gutter on desktop, 16px on mobile
